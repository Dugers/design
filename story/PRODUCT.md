# Некоторые совпадения

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, explicitly chosen by the user for the first prototype. Published with the studio through a copying build; no third-party runtime.

## Users
Katya, exploring a mysterious personal story after discovering a printed QR code. Anton authors and updates the story.

## Product Purpose
A personal interactive story spanning October and November 2026. Real encounters change earlier chapters, and digital clues foreshadow later experiences. This first version establishes the visual design and expandable event entries.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- The opening must read: «Некоторые события кажутся случайными лишь до тех пор, пока не становятся частью чего-то большего».
- Event dates may be visible or partially masked, for example x1.11.2026 or xx.11.2026.
- Small category icons hint at the nature of an event.
- Some entries expand inline to reveal more text.
- Authorized history: 25.09.2026 first online messages; 04.10 first in-person meeting; 08.10 argument; 09.10 reconciliation; 10.10 first kiss and mutual declaration. Do not publish private specifics about other contacts.
- Preserve «Чуть теплее, чем обычно» with 23.10.2026. «Когда темнота не страшная» uses xx.11.2026. A title-hidden, icon-free locked row uses 0x.11.2026. «Поправка к легенде» uses xx.11.2026 and hints at the future personal question through an unfinished map legend; its second layer unfolds natively. «Продолжение следует» uses xx.xx.xxxx.
- Chapter wording is provisional; event count and dates must not dominate.
- Public chapter copy must hint through images and motifs, never directly recount messages, meetings, arguments, reconciliation or declarations. Preserve the entire «Поправка к легенде» chapter verbatim; the user explicitly approved its wording. Every visible chapter icon should be distinct; the hidden-title row has no icon.
- Future chapters may have distinct visual identities and change over time. Scheduling, editing and persistence are outside this prototype.
- Preserve mystery and avoid revealing the dinner, Halloween or final relationship question.
- The eventual relationship question is delivered in person, with no required answer or progression gate on the site.

## Brand Commitments
Dark, minimalist, visibly hand-drawn. The supplied avatar establishes dusty pink, brown, cream, soft irregular outlines and a small star motif. Intimate, curious and lightly humorous; restrained romance, no sentimental clichés.
